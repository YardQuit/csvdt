Converts a rfc3339 timestamp in the given column [0...] to an
explicit UTC offset that you supply, comma-joined with no space
(e.g. -o1,-06:00, -o1,+0200, -o1,Z). Unlike -l/--local, this
doesn't depend on the timezone the machine running csvdt happens
to be configured for. The offset you supply must be one a real
place uses: -12:00 through +14:00.

Anything beyond that is refused rather than converted, so a typo
like +15:00 for +05:00 is reported instead of quietly producing
a timestamp with an offset that cannot exist. Offsets already
present in the data are read as they are and not
range-checked -- those are the input's business, not a mistake
in the command.
