A command-line tool that converts the timestamps in CSV files
and rearranges their columns. It reads and writes CSV with
whatever whitespace, quoting and delimiters a file needs, and is
made for data analysis, where a value has to come out as the
source had it or be marked as one that could not be converted.

Duration results (-d/--duration) are deliberately reported only
in days, hours, minutes, and seconds -- never years, months, or
weeks. Those units have no fixed length (a month is 28-31 days,
a year 365-366), so expressing a duration in them would mean
silently rounding to some average length. This tool never
approximates a duration: every value is exact and reproducible,
which matters when it may be relied on as evidence, e.g. in an
investigation.
