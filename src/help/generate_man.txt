Writes this program's manual page to standard output, in the
roff source a man page is made of, and exits. Rendered from the
same option definitions that parse a command line, so it
describes the build it came out of and cannot fall behind it.
That is why it is generated rather than written: a page kept by
hand is a second copy of every option, and the one that goes
stale. For packagers, who need a page at the moment they build:

|    csvdt --generate-man > csvdt.1

The RPM installs a page this way. Every other install can have
one for a command and a redirect, since the option prints roff
and does nothing else:

|    mkdir -p ~/.local/share/man/man1
|    csvdt --generate-man > ~/.local/share/man/man1/csvdt.1
|    man csvdt

That directory is on the default manpath on most systems; where
it is not, MANPATH covers it. System-wide is the same line into
/usr/local/share/man/man1/, then mandb where a distribution
caches. Worth it for the reading rather than the reference: the
page adapts to the terminal and arrives in a pager, where this
help never rewraps, so KNOWN LIMITS or EXIT STATUS is a search
away rather than a scroll.
