The field delimiter to use when printing (writing) CSV.

[default: ',']
One ASCII character, as for --read-delimiter, and refused on the
same grounds: a newline or carriage return ends a record rather
than dividing one, and '"' is what the writer quotes with.
Given '"' the writer produced output that could not be read
back -- the row x"y,2 came out as "x""y""2, which reads as one
field rather than two.
