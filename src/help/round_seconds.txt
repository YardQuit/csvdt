Rounds -d/--duration's result to whole seconds, half-up,
dropping any sub-second part (e.g. PT1.6S becomes PT2S and
PT0.4S becomes PT0S). Only affects --duration, and only when you
ask for it: by default a duration keeps the full sub-second
precision its timestamps carried, so nothing is approximated
unless you opt in here. Useful when the source timestamps carry
more precision than the question does, and an
exact-to-the-nanosecond span would only be noise.
