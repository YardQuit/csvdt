Placement of an action's inserted/replaced value: a column
[0...] and one of before/after/replace, comma-joined with no
space (e.g. -i1,replace). If omitted, defaults to placing it
right after the action's own column (or its second column, for a
two-column --duration). This column is never out of bound: too
high clamps to the last column, and a negative value (e.g.
-i-1,replace) clamps to the first column (0).
