Removes columns from the CSV output. Takes one column [0...], or
several comma-joined with no space (e.g. --remove 0,3,4). The
numbers refer to positions in the input, so they don't shift as
earlier columns are dropped: --remove 0,1 removes the first two
columns, not the first and then whatever moved into second
place.

Order doesn't matter. A column named twice is refused rather
than removed once: the list would not be saying what it looks
like, and -a/--arrange refuses a repeat for its own reasons, so
both lists now answer the same mistake the same way. Every
column must exist, or the row is reported as out of bound rather
than partly removed.
